Who we are

Hope in Action

Umeed Foundation (उम्मीद फाउंडेशन) is a community-led NGO dedicated to creating lasting change.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through access to education, healthcare, dignity and opportunity.

Our Vision

A future where every person can live with equality, confidence and hope.

2010 — A small beginning

Umeed began with a group of citizens teaching children in one neighbourhood.

2016 — Growing together

Healthcare camps and women-led livelihood groups expanded our work.

Today — Hope at scale

Thousands of families now benefit from our integrated community programs.
